# Documentation: `src/point-in-time_Universe.py`

## Overview
`src/point-in-time_Universe.py` is the point-in-time universe construction engine. It creates a dynamic boolean masking matrix $M_{t,i}$ across all trading days $t$ and constituents $i$. Its primary econometric function is to guarantee that the backtesting engine remains strictly free from **look-ahead bias** and **survivorship bias**.

---

## Mathematical Formulation

Let $\mathcal{T} = \{t_1, t_2, \dots, t_T\}$ denote the ordered discrete set of valid U.S. trading days derived from the historical price database index. Let $\mathcal{U} = \{S_1, S_2, \dots, S_N\}$ denote the universe of all evaluated corporate entities.

For each asset $i \in \mathcal{U}$, let $t_{\text{start}, i}$ and $t_{\text{end}, i}$ represent the verified tenure timestamps during which a Black Chief Executive Officer held office.

The investability indicator $M_{t, i} \in \{0, 1\}$ is defined as:

$$M_{t, i} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } t_{\text{start}, i} \le t \le t_{\text{end}, i} \\  0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

The active constituent count on any trading day $t$ is:

$$N_t = \sum_{i=1}^{N} M_{t, i}$$

## Function Reference

### `create_point_in_time_mask(roster_path, prices_path)`

#### Inputs
* **`roster_path`** (`str`, default=`"data/black_ceo_roster.csv"`):  
  Path to the verified corporate tenure ledger. Expected columns:
  * `ticker` (`str`): Equity ticker symbol.
  * `company_name` (`str`): Entity name.
  * `ceo_name` (`str`): Executive name.
  * `start_date` (`datetime`): Tenure start date.
  * `end_date` (`datetime`): Tenure end date.
  * `sector` (`str`): GICS sector classification.
* **`prices_path`** (`str`, default=`"data/prices_daily.csv"`):  
  Path to the cached historical adjusted closing price matrix generated by `src/data_loader.py`.

#### Outputs
Returns a tuple of three Pandas objects: `(stock_prices, mask, benchmarks)`
1. **`stock_prices`** (`pd.DataFrame`, shape: $T \times N$):  
   Daily adjusted closing prices for non-benchmark equity tickers.
2. **`mask`** (`pd.DataFrame`, shape: $T \times N$):  
   Boolean matrix where `mask.iloc[t, i] == True` if asset $i$ is actively eligible for capital allocation on date $t$.
3. **`benchmarks`** (`pd.DataFrame`, shape: $T \times 2$):  
   Daily price series for market benchmarks (`SPY` and `RSP`).

---

## Edge Case & Bias Mitigation

1. **Look-Ahead Bias Elimination:**  
   Assets are never included prior to their verified appointment date $t_{\text{start}, i}$.
2. **Survivorship Bias Handling:**  
   Companies where the CEO stepped down or the company underwent restructuring remain in the historical mask throughout their active tenure and exit immediately on $t_{\text{end}, i}$.
3. **Calendar Alignment:**  
   Tenure start and end dates falling on weekends or exchange holidays are automatically resolved to the next/prior valid trading day via datetime inequality slicing (`mask.index >= start_dt`).
4. **Low-Constituent Regimes ($N_t < 3$):**  
   The module records $N_t$ so downstream weighting engines can implement defensive cash-drag allocation when the active pool is too concentrated.
